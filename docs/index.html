How the numbers are made

Panels, authors, and dates are read from the court's opinions by fixed text rules. Issues and which side each ruling favored were coded by AI and checked against a random sample. Rule 36 judgments, en banc decisions, and (by default) pro se cases are left out of the vote counts. Read the full methodology, including the list of issues, how sides are defined, and the known limits.
