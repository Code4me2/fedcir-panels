Report a problem

Something not loading, a number that looks wrong, or a decision the AI coded incorrectly? Please let us know by email.

What to include

  • The link to the page you were looking at. The dashboard's address records the session, judges, case type, and issue you chose, so the link shows exactly what you saw. If you came here from the dashboard's menu, the email button above adds it for you.
  • What looks wrong, and what you expected instead.
  • For a decision coded incorrectly: the case name or appeal number, the issue, and what the opinion actually held. A pin cite helps.
  • For something broken: your browser and device (for example, Safari on an iPhone), and a screenshot if you can.

Before you write

  • Issues and outcomes are coded by AI from the court's opinions, and some errors are expected. The methodology explains the rules and known limits, which may explain a number that looks surprising.
  • For an upcoming session, the judges are entered by you; the court posts the actual panel about one hour before argument.
  • New decisions are added every week, so a decision from the last few days may not be included yet.

This site is not affiliated with the court. Please don't send confidential client information.

Source: opinions, Rule 36 judgments, argument recordings, and calendars posted by the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit on its website. Not affiliated with the court. Not legal advice.
